Your tasks,
in the terminal.

tasq is a fast, keyboard-first to-do app. Type tasks the way you'd say them, see your day and your week, and keep your calendars in step. Local-first, free and open source.

$ brew trust tasq-app/tap
$ brew install tasq-app/tap/tasq
$ tasq
$ git clone https://github.com/tasq-app/tasq
$ cd tasq && ./install.sh

macOS and Linux · early alpha: keep your todo.txt around, tasq only reads it.

Features

Everything a day needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

A list, a calendar and a capture box that understands you, in a terminal app that opens instantly and never leaves the keyboard.

Type it like you say it.

Write exam friday at 9am for 2h in exams and tasq picks out the day, the time, the duration and the space as you type, each as a chip you can fix.

  • Repeats: “every other monday”, “every mon and fri for 6 weeks”
  • Deadlines, reminders, priorities, tags
  • Spaces and subspaces: +Uni/Exams

Your day, at a glance.

Today shows what's overdue, then your morning, afternoon and evening in order, with the free time between your blocks.

  • Today · Upcoming · All, plus an inbox for the undated
  • Filters as chips, search across tasks and notes
  • Checklists and Markdown notes on any task

Plan the week. See the month.

Day, week and month views over the same tasks. Drag a block to move it, stretch it to change how long it takes, and scroll the month a week at a time.

  • Time blocks or an agenda, a key away
  • Multi-day events across their days
  • Edit one occurrence of a repeat, or all of them

…y también en español.

Capture understands Spanish too: llamar al dentista mañana a las 6 cada lunes. More languages follow the same way.

Connected calendars

In Google & Apple Calendar,
in your colours.

Connect once from Settings. Your dated tasks show up next to your meetings, each in its space's colour, and stay in step as you work. They go straight from your computer to Google or iCloud, with no server of ours in between.

  • Google Calendar: one “tasq” calendar, each event in the nearest Google colour to its space
  • Apple Calendar: a calendar per space (“tasq · Uni”), in its colour, over iCloud CalDAV
  • Timed blocks, all-day and multi-day events, repeats and reminders
  • Only what changed is sent, a moment after you change it
